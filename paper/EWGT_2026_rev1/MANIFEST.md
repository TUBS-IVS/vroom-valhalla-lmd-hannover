# EWGT 2026 — Revision 1 (Preprint, Under Review)

Revised version of the EWGT 2026 paper, answering the reviewer comments on the
2026-05-31 submission:

> *Bienzeisler, L., Petre, F., Wage, O., and Friedrich, B.*, **"Machine-Learning
> Surrogate Optimization for Time-Based Consolidation in Last-Mile Parcel
> Delivery"**, submitted to Transportation Research Procedia (EWGT 2026).
> **Manuscript currently under review — not yet accepted.**

The submitted version is preserved unchanged in
[`../EWGT_2026/`](../EWGT_2026/). This folder is the revision, and the two are
meant to be diffed against each other.

**What is public here.** The revision was drafted in the Elsevier camera-ready
layout, which is the version under review at the venue and is *not* published:
`elsevier_source/` is gitignored. The public artefact is the same content in the
preprint layout (`elsarticle` with the `preprint` option), exactly as for the
submitted version — `tbc_preprint_main.tex` plus its build, shipped as
`EWGT26_Full_Paper_LB_preprint.pdf`. The two layouts are kept in content
agreement; `scripts/revision/` regenerates everything either of them cites.

## What changed against the submission

Full reviewer-by-reviewer account in [`CHANGES_rev1.md`](CHANGES_rev1.md). The
scientific substance of the change is that all optimisation results were
recomputed on the **Stage-3** schedules (per-hub balancing followed by
system-wide fleet smoothing) instead of the Stage-2 balanced schedules, and the
VROOM revalidation was extended from three to four operating points.

| Quantity | Submitted | Revision 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Validated operating points | 3 (P ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5}) | **4** (adds P = 0.75) |
| Surrogate conservatism band | 1.3 – 2.1 pp | **0.9 – 2.7 pp** |
| Realised saving at P = 0 | 24.3 % | **23.7 %** |
| Realised saving at P = 0.25 | — | **19.8 %** (predicted 18.5 %) |
| Realised saving at P = 0.75 | — | **13.0 %** (predicted 10.2 %) |
| Added wait at P = 0.25 | 0.45 d | **0.46 d** |
| Monday–Saturday fleet CV reduction | up to 60 % / 71 % | **54 % at (0.5, 1), up to 78 % across the grid** |
| Efficient-range saving in the conclusion | "up to 22.8 %" | **13.5 – 18.5 %, up to 22.8 % at P = 0** |

Figures 4, 5 and 6 and all six tables were regenerated on the Stage-3 basis.
Figures 1, 2 and 3 are unchanged from the submission — they describe input data
and methodology, which the recomputation does not touch.

## How to compile

```powershell
cd paper/EWGT_2026_rev1

# Tectonic (single binary, fetches packages on demand, resolves bib and
# cross-references in one pass):
tectonic -X compile tbc_preprint_main.tex
Copy-Item tbc_preprint_main.pdf EWGT26_Full_Paper_LB_preprint.pdf -Force

# Or a classic TeX distribution:
pdflatex tbc_preprint_main; bibtex tbc_preprint_main
pdflatex tbc_preprint_main; pdflatex tbc_preprint_main
```

`\input@path` points at `build_assets/` for the class files and
`\graphicspath` at `figures/`; both are declared in the preamble. Build
artefacts (`.aux`, `.bbl`, `.log`, …) are gitignored.

## Reproducing the revision

The Stage-3 recomputation runs from the checkpoints, CPU-only, and is
resumable. From the repository root, in order:

```powershell
python scripts/revision/00_regen_checkpoints.py       # stages 1+3 checkpoints
python scripts/revision/10_recompute_stage3_outputs.py # 88-cell grid, ~1-2 h
python scripts/revision/20_validate_vroom_smoothed.py  # needs VROOM + Valhalla
python scripts/revision/21_pstar_knees_smoothed.py
python scripts/revision/30_fig5_heatmap_smoothed.py
python scripts/revision/31_fig6_structural_smoothed.py
python scripts/revision/32_fig4_mix.py
python scripts/revision/40_tables_smoothed.py
python scripts/revision/41_op_kpi_tables_smoothed.py
```

Every stage carries verification gates that abort on accounting drift rather
than emitting a plausible wrong number — the Stage-2 fleet reproduction and
system-spread checks in `10_`, the knee recomputation in `21_`, the
frequency-invariance assertion in `32_`, and the reconciliation of the
operational KPI totals against the published validation savings in `41_`.
