# EWGT 2026 — Submitted Paper Outputs Manifest

Frozen byte-identical copies of every figure and table that appears in the
submitted paper:

> *Bienzeisler, L. and Friedrich, B.*, **"Machine-Learning Surrogate
> Optimization for Time-Based Consolidation in Last-Mile Parcel Delivery"**,
> Transportation Research Procedia (EWGT 2026), preprint 2026-05-31.

This manifest maps every numerical claim in the manuscript to the figure or
table that backs it AND to the script that produced it. Numbers are taken
verbatim from the submission abstract / results section.
