# `data/` — Input data (not tracked in git)

This folder is **gitignored** because the parcel-demand shapefiles
(generated by our sister project [HAGRID](https://github.com/TUBS-IVS/HAGRID))
and the Region Hannover postal-code geodata are large (~400 MB) and
have their own licensing terms that prevent in-line redistribution.

## Layout (expected once populated)

```
data/
├── README.md            ← this file (tracked)
├── MANIFEST.md          ← input-data inventory (tracked)
├── demand/              HAGRID parcel-demand shapefiles
│   └── *.shp + .shx + .dbf + .prj (per-weekday subsets)
├── geodata/             Region Hannover spatial data
│   ├── region_hannover.geojson
│   ├── plz_areas.shp + sidecars
│   ├── cluster_raumtyp.csv
│   └── plz_raumtyp.csv
├── hubs/                LSP hub/depot CSVs
│   └── kep_hubs.csv
└── vehicles/            HAGRID vehicle type definitions
    └── hagrid_vehicle_types.csv
```

## Obtaining the data

The parcel-demand shapefiles under `data/demand/` are produced by
**[HAGRID](https://github.com/TUBS-IVS/HAGRID)** — our companion
demand-generation pipeline, developed by the same author group at the
TU Braunschweig *Institute of Transportation and Urban Engineering*.
HAGRID takes regional census + commercial-flows data and synthesises
weekly per-postal-code, per-LSP parcel volumes. The exact snapshot used
for the EWGT 2026 paper is the May 2025 run.

To obtain the exact data bundle used in the paper (HAGRID output + PLZ
geometry + hub CSVs), please contact the corresponding author
(lasse.bienzeisler@tu-braunschweig.de). A future commit will replace
this manual step with a `scripts/data/download_inputs.py` script that
fetches the inputs from a Zenodo DOI, but at the time of paper
submission the data are not yet mirrored to a public archive.

proprietary commercial-flows inputs and is therefore shared for
